Privacy Policy

Reapers Community Clips monitors creator accounts for new public videos about Sea of Thieves and sends selected video links to Discord channels. TikTok authorization is not yet available on this website.

Information used

  • Creator account names, public profile images and public video details, including links, titles, descriptions, tags and upload times.
  • Discord server, channel and optional member identifiers, notification preferences, and records of which video links were posted to avoid duplicate alerts.
  • If TikTok account connection becomes available, authorization tokens needed to read the connected account's permitted public video information. The requested permissions will be explained in TikTok's consent screen.

Purpose and sharing

We use this information to check for new videos, match Sea of Thieves content, display creator information, publish notifications, prevent duplicates and troubleshoot errors. Video links and chosen public profile details are shared in the designated Discord channels. TikTok and Discord process information under their own policies. We do not sell creator account information.

Retention and removal

Source configuration remains while an account is listed for monitoring. Removing an account stops future checks, but earlier Discord messages and duplicate-prevention records may remain. To request deletion of related stored records or removal of existing bot messages, contact the Reapers Community team through a support ticket in the Reapers Community Discord server where the bot operates. If TikTok connection is added, you may also revoke access in TikTok's connected-app settings.

Security and changes

Access to bot configuration and stored data is limited to the team operating the service. We may update this policy as the service changes and post the updated effective date here.

Contact

Open a support ticket in the Reapers Community Discord server where the bot operates. See our Terms of Service.
