Creator video notifications

Sea of Thieves clips, shared with the community.

Reapers Community Clips is a Discord notification service that checks connected creator accounts for new public videos and shares clips about Sea of Thieves in designated community channels.

How it works

Server administrators add creator accounts to the clips system. The service checks recent public videos, looks for Sea of Thieves in the title, description or tags, and posts matching video links in Discord. TikTok account authorization and its official video access are being prepared; no TikTok account is connected through this website today.

Creators keep ownership of their content. Reapers Community Clips links to videos on their original platforms.

Questions or removal requests

Contact the Reapers Community team through a support ticket in the Reapers Community Discord server where this bot operates. A server administrator can also remove an account from monitoring with /clips remove.
