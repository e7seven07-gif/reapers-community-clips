Terms of Service

These terms apply to Reapers Community Clips, a Discord service operated by the Reapers Community team for sharing creator video links about Sea of Thieves.

Using the service

Discord server administrators may add or remove creator accounts and choose where matching video notifications appear. The service may check recent public video information and publish links to matching videos. We may pause monitoring or remove a source when the service is unavailable, a creator requests removal, or continued use would violate platform rules or another person's rights.

Creators and permissions

Creators retain all rights in their videos. This service does not claim ownership of videos or provide downloads. Connecting a TikTok account, when this feature becomes available, will require the account holder's authorization. Account holders may revoke permission through TikTok's connected-app settings and request removal from our monitoring.

Accuracy and availability

Game matching uses information associated with public videos, such as titles, descriptions and tags. It may miss a relevant clip or include an unrelated one. Notifications may be delayed when a platform or the service is unavailable. We may change or stop the service.

Questions

Contact the Reapers Community team through a support ticket in the Reapers Community Discord server where the bot operates. Also see our Privacy Policy.
